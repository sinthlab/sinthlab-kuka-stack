Cue board

The NeoPixel ring is driven by the Metro board in the end effector. This page talks to it over the board's own Wi-Fi: join this computer to KUKA_NEOPIXEL first. Its address on that network is always 192.168.4.1.

What a cue looks like

Brightness is not capped on the board. Above ~0.3 with colour (not white) the ring can draw more than the effector's 5 V converter supplies — see the POWER note in end_effector_metro_code/code.py.

LED-by-LED test

Lights each LED red, green, blue, white in turn. Where the ring goes dark is the fault: the ring is 4 arcs of 15 (pixels 0–14, 15–29, 30–44, 45–59). Watch the pixel number below.
